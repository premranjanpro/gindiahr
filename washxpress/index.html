Site & Utility Checklist

Franchise Store Requirements

Everything needed to get your commercial dry cleaning & laundry outlet operational in your city.

Carpet Area & Location

Minimum 400 to 600 sq. ft. carpet area in a prominent market or dense residential neighborhood with high visibility and customer access.

Electricity Power Load

1-phase or 3-phase commercial electrical connection with a minimum 15 KVA to 30 KVA load capacity to run industrial washers, dryers and boilers.

Water & Drainage

Daily supply of 1000 litres water with <500 TDS, along with dedicated drainage channels and fresh exhaust ventilation piping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for aspiring entrepreneurs looking for laundry franchises in Patna, Bihar, and India.

What is the minimum setup cost for a Washxpress franchise in Patna?
The entry-level Spin GO Model starts at ₹14.98 Lakhs (net all-inclusive). This includes complete commercial LG wet-cleaning washers & dryers, Fabcare steam pressing tables, automatic 6KW steam boiler, 15KVA voltage stabilizer, computers, POS CRM, tag printers, full interior decoration with AC & CCTV, and the franchise fee.
Do you provide laundry services or franchise business setups?
Washxpress is a B2B Turnkey Franchise Provider. We help entrepreneurs build, launch, and operate their own profitable commercial laundry & dry cleaning stores. From location audit, shop interior, machine delivery, staff recruitment & training to opening day marketing, we deliver a 100% operational store.
How long does it take from the 1st call to the grand store opening?
Under our streamlined 10-step process, store setup is completed in approximately 30 to 35 days from the signing of the LOI and location finalization.
How does the Zero-Royalty policy work?
We charge ₹0 Royalty until your store achieves monthly sales of ₹1,00,000 and above. Once breakeven is reached, royalty is a low flat monthly fee (₹4,000/mo in Year 1, ₹7,000/mo in Year 2, ₹10,000/mo in Year 3 onwards) rather than heavy percentage cuts.
Do you assist with staff hiring and machine operation training?
Yes! Through our GIndiaHR parent recruitment infrastructure, we assist in hiring experienced washermen, dry cleaning experts, ironing masters, and store executives. We provide hands-on SOP training, garment tagging training, chemical ratios, and software management.
