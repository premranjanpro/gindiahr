By Usha Singh • 6 Min Read • Executive Leadership

Hiring a Chief Executive Officer, Managing Director, Chief Technology Officer, or Manufacturing Plant Head requires a completely different approach from standard contingency recruitment. Top leaders are rarely active on public job portals; they are passive candidates who must be confidentially headhunted based on trust, vision alignment, and bespoke compensation structuring.

1. The 7-Day Retained Search Framework by GIndiaHR

  • Day 1–2: Competitor Mapping & Role Profiling: Deep dive into the corporate mandate, performance KPIs, reporting matrix, and compensation benchmarks.
  • Day 3–4: Discrete Headhunting & Direct Reach-Out: Confidential engagement with top 1% sector leaders across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, and Kolkata.
  • Day 5–6: Leadership Competency & Culture Assessment: Comprehensive technical, behavioral, and background vetting conducted directly by executive partners.
  • Day 7: Presentation of 3 Vetted Finalists: Delivery of structured executive dossiers with interview availability for the Board of Directors.
