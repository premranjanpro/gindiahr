Washxpress Franchise Models Compared: Spin GO vs Pure Wash vs Luxe Care vs Door Wash

By Usha Singh • 7 Min Read • Financial Analysis

Choosing the right commercial laundry franchise model depends on three primary parameters: available commercial carpet area, local catchment demographics, and total investment budget.

Washxpress provides four distinct turnkey packages ranging from ₹14.98 Lakhs to ₹28.02 Lakhs. Below is the comprehensive technical and operational comparison.

1. Model 1: Spin GO (₹14.98 Lakh) — Compact Express Model

Designed for compact shop spaces in high-density student areas, coaching hubs, and residential societies.

  • Space Requirement: 250 to 350 Sq.Ft.
  • Machinery: 2 Heavy-Duty LG Commercial Washers (13 Kg) + 2 Commercial Stack Dryers (13 Kg).
  • Daily Processing Capacity: 120–150 Kg laundry daily.
  • Projected Monthly Gross Revenue: ₹1.4 Lakh to ₹2.1 Lakh.
  • Target Breakeven: 10 to 12 Months.

2. Model 2: Pure Wash (₹17.75 Lakh) — Most Popular Retail Laundromat

The standard flagship high-street store model delivering express wash, fold, and professional steam ironing.

  • Space Requirement: 400 to 600 Sq.Ft.
  • Machinery: 3 LG Commercial Washers + 3 Dryers + 1 Commercial Vacuum Steam Ironing Table with Boiler.
  • Daily Capacity: 250–320 Kg laundry daily.
  • Projected Monthly Gross Revenue: ₹2.5 Lakh to ₹3.8 Lakh.
  • Target Breakeven: 12 to 14 Months.

3. Model 3: Luxe Care (₹21.38 Lakh) — Full Dry Cleaning & Premium Garment Care

Engineered for high-end residential neighbourhoods catering to designer wear, heavy winterwear, and wedding silks.

  • Space Requirement: 600 to 900 Sq.Ft.
  • Machinery: Commercial Wet Cleaning System + Italian Hydrocarbon Dry Cleaning Plant + Automatic Spotting Table + Boiler Press.
  • Projected Monthly Gross Revenue: ₹3.5 Lakh to ₹5.2 Lakh.
  • Target Breakeven: 14 to 16 Months.

4. Model 4: Door Wash (₹28.02 Lakh) — Central Hub & Mobile Electric Delivery Fleet

App-driven on-demand pickup and delivery hub serving corporate campuses, hospitals, hotels, and multiple city pin codes.

  • Setup: Semi-Industrial Central Plant + Branded Electric Delivery Two-Wheelers/Vans + GPS POS Routing Software.
  • Projected Monthly Gross Revenue: ₹5.0 Lakh to ₹8.5 Lakh.
  • Target Breakeven: 15 to 18 Months.
