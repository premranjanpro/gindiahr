Mastering Resume Screening & Shortlisting: A Recruiter's Playbook

Published by Global India HR Solutions | Talent Desk: +91 6202254762

On average, every corporate job opening receives over 250 resumes. Efficient resume screening is the single most important step to saving time and securing top 5% talent before competitors do.

1. Define Must-Have vs Nice-to-Have Criteria

Before reviewing a single CV, clearly split the job requirement. Must-Haves include required tech stack, mandatory years of experience, and location availability. Nice-to-Haves include secondary tools or niche industry experience.

2. Watch Out for Achievement Metrics (Not Just Job Duties)

Strong candidates highlight quantifiable results (e.g. "Increased sales revenue by 35%", "Reduced API latency by 40ms"). Be cautious of resumes listing only vague responsibilities.

3. Spot Red Flags Intelligently

Evaluate career gaps or frequent job switching objectively by focusing on verified accomplishments and references rather than immediate rejection.
